GDCOC counters crimes committed by organised crime groups (OCGs). What is an OCG?

Required data

When submitting a report, please provide:

  • Your names
  • Date of birth
  • Telephone — for fast and efficient communication
  • Email for contact
  • Current address
  • Company name and ЕИК (Unified Identification Code) *

Describe the case accurately, clearly and chronologically, attaching files and other materials that may assist the investigation.

* Required only when the victim or sender is a legal entity (company or organisation).
