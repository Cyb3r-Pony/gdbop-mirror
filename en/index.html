Combating Organised Crime

We counter organised crime on the territory of the Republic of Bulgaria and beyond its borders — in coordination with Europol, Interpol and partner services.

Mission

In Service of Society

“Prevention, detection and neutralisation of organised crime groups — with professionalism, legality and resolve.”

GDCOC is the national specialised structure of the Ministry of the Interior for operational, detection, information-analysis and organisational activity in preventing, disrupting, investigating and detecting the organised criminal activity of local and transnational criminal structures.

On the front line since 1991
Areas

Areas of Work

A range of specialised areas covering the full spectrum of countering organised crime.

01

Drugs

Countering the production, trafficking and distribution of narcotic substances and precursors.

 02

Trafficking in Cultural Valuables

Protecting cultural heritage and disrupting the illicit trafficking of antiquities and valuables.

 03

Cybercrime

Investigating computer crime, online fraud and abuses in cyberspace.

 04

Human Trafficking

Countering trafficking in human beings for exploitation and protecting the victims.

 05

Migrant Smuggling

Countering organised channels for the illegal movement and smuggling of migrants.

 06

Counter-Terrorism

Preventing and countering terrorism, radicalisation and its financing.

 07

Corruption

Uncovering corruption schemes and crimes by officials at the highest levels of government.

 08

Money Laundering

Uncovering money-laundering schemes and tracing assets acquired through criminal activity.

 09

Crimes against the tax and social-security system

Countering tax and social-security fraud — evasion of liabilities and VAT schemes.

 10

Counterfeiting

Countering the counterfeiting of currency, documents and payment instruments.

 11

Smuggling

Disrupting smuggling channels for excise goods, weapons and dual-use goods.

 12

Investigation

Pre-trial proceedings and procedural actions in organised crime cases.

 13

Inter-agency Coordination Centre

Coordination between institutions and real-time exchange of operational information.
